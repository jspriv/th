Nepali dishes: what to eat and limit

Your daily Nepali diet plan

7:00–8:00 AM — Breakfast
  • 2 boiled eggs
  • 1–2 rotis or a bowl of oats
  • 1 glass of milk or a small bowl of plain dahi
  • 1 seasonal fruit

If you take levothyroxine, take it with water on an empty stomach and wait 30–60 minutes before eating.

12:00–1:30 PM — Lunch
  • Rice (bhat)
  • 1 bowl of dal
  • 1 serving of seasonal tarkari or green saag
  • Chicken or lean buff, about a palm-sized portion
  • Cucumber or carrot salad
3:30–4:30 PM — Snack
  • Roasted chana or a small handful of nuts
  • Guava, apple, orange, or another seasonal fruit
  • Water or unsweetened tea
6:30–8:00 PM — Dinner
  • 2 rotis or a moderate serving of rice
  • Fish, chicken, paneer, or dal
  • Cooked vegetables
  • Plain dahi if desired
9:00 PM — Optional
  • A glass of milk if you are hungry.
  • If you take thyroid medicine, keep milk and calcium supplements away from your medication according to your clinician’s instructions.

Vitamin D & calcium-rich foods

Thyroid problems can weaken bones over time, so both nutrients matter for either type.

Vitamin DOily fish, egg yolks, UV-grown mushrooms, fortified milk

Salmon, mackerel, sardines and whole eggs are the best food sources. Fish has iodine, so keep it moderate with an overactive thyroid. Morning sunlight for 10–15 minutes also helps; ask your doctor about a supplement if you are low.

CalciumMilk, curd, paneer, chhurpi

The easiest sources. With an underactive thyroid, keep dairy at least 4 hours from levothyroxine. With an overactive thyroid, dairy has some iodine, so moderate portions are best.

CalciumSesame (til), almonds, chickpeas, beans, dal

Dairy-free options that also bring zinc and fibre. Eat them away from your pill if you take one.

CalciumLeafy greens: mustard greens (rayo saag), methi, kale

Good calcium. Cook cruciferous greens like kale and mustard; spinach has calcium that is poorly absorbed.

CalciumSmall fish eaten with bones (sardines)

Calcium and vitamin D together. Moderate portions because of iodine.

CarefulRagi / millet, calcium-set tofu

Ragi is high in calcium but millet can act as a goitrogen in large amounts; tofu may reduce pill absorption. Occasional servings are fine, away from your pill.

CarefulCalcium & iron supplements

Take 4 hours apart from levothyroxine. Ask your doctor or pharmacist before starting any supplement.

Which meat is better?

BestSkinless chicken & eggs

Lean, with zinc and selenium. Easy to cook with little oil (boiled, grilled, light curry).

BestLean goat (khasi) & buff

Good zinc, iron and B12. Trim the fat and keep portions palm-sized, 2–3 times a week. Fatty cuts add to weight gain.

ModerateFish

Great for selenium and vitamin D. Fine 2–3 times a week with an underactive thyroid, but limit with an overactive one because of iodine.

ModeratePork & beef

Fine if lean; fatty cuts and bacon are not helpful.

AvoidProcessed meat

Sausage, salami, bacon, packaged nuggets: high in salt and fat, and Healthline advises avoiding highly processed foods. Deep-fried meat is also best occasional.

1-month exercise routine

Gentle and progressive, written for an underactive thyroid. Aim for “moderate”: you can still talk while moving. Get your doctor’s okay before you start.

Week 1Start easy
  • Walk 20 minutes, 5 days
  • 5 minutes of gentle stretching daily
  • 2 rest days
Week 2Add light strength
  • Walk 30 minutes, 5 days
  • 2 days of bodyweight strength, 15 minutes: wall push-ups, chair squats, glute bridges (2 sets of 8–10)
  • 10 minutes of gentle yoga on one day
Week 3Build a little
  • Walk 30–35 minutes, 5 days, with 2–3 brisk minutes mixed in
  • 2–3 days of strength, 20 minutes: add lunges and leg raises (2–3 sets of 10)
  • 15 minutes of yoga or pilates: cat-cow, child’s pose, bridge
Week 4Settle into a routine
  • Walk or cycle 35–40 minutes, 5 days, with a few brisk stretches
  • 3 days of strength, 25 minutes
  • 1 yoga or stretching day and 1 full rest day
Good habitsFor every week
  • Take levothyroxine on an empty stomach and exercise after your breakfast gap
  • Drink water and eat a small snack before longer sessions
  • Skip pre-workout supplements and energy drinks (caffeine)
  • On low-energy days, do half the plan instead of skipping everything
  • Sleep 7–8 hours; recovery matters
If overactiveKeep it lower intensity

Stick to walking, gentle yoga, swimming and light weights. Avoid HIIT, sprinting and heavy lifting until your doctor says your thyroid is controlled. Use the Week 1–2 pace for the whole month.

StopWarning signs

Chest pain, an irregular or very fast heartbeat, fainting, or breathlessness at rest: stop and seek medical care. Don’t change your medicine on your own, and ask your doctor when to repeat your TSH test.
